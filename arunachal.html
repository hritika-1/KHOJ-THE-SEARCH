Arunachal Pradesh is a state in northeast India.The capital of Arunachal Pradesh is Itanagar.Arunachal Pradesh is famous as the "Land of the Dawn-Lit Mountains" for its breathtaking Himalayan landscapes, rich indigenous tribal culture, and spiritual Buddhist landmarks. Its international borders include Bhutan, China and Myanmar whereas its domestic borders include Assam and Nagaland.

It is the fourteenth largest state in India by area. English is the official language of this state. Its indigenour languages also include Nyishi, Adi, Apatani, Tagin, Galo, Khamti and many more.
