The

Daman and Diu is a union territory in western India, located along the Arabian Sea. It consists of two distinct regions: Daman and Diu. Gujarat is the land border for this union territory.Daman and Diu is famous for its stunning beaches, rich Portuguese colonial history, and massive sea-facing forts.

The union territory was merged with Dadra and Nagar Haveli in 2020.Daman serves as the capital and administrative headquarters for the unified territory, while Silvassa functions as another major administrative hub and the largest city. Gujarat is the most widely spoken language in this region.
