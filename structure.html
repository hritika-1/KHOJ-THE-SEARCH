Telangana is a state in southern India on the Deccan Plateau.The capital of Telangana is Hyderabad. It borders 4 states - Maharashtra, Chhattisgarh, Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka.

It is the eleventh largest state in India by area. Telugu and Urdu are the official languages of this state. It is known for its unique blend of modern and traditional culture.
