Chattisgarh is a state in central India.The capital of Chhattisgarh is Raipur.Chhattisgarh is famous for its rich tribal culture, dense forests, stunning waterfalls, and major industrial hubs. It borders Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Jharkhand, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana.

It is ninth largest state in India by area. The official languages of this state are Hindi and Chhattisgarhi.
